Geography studies maps, continents, oceans, regions, climates, coasts, islands,
and locations. This starter topic is about continents and oceans.

Antarctica and the Antarctic region are cold. The Arctic Ocean is near the north
pole. The Atlantic Ocean and the Indian Ocean are major oceans. Oceania is a
region with many islands. A continent is a very large area of land. An ocean is a
very large body of water.

Topic answer words: Antarctica, Arctic Ocean, Atlantic, Indian Ocean, Oceania,
Continent, Ocean, Antarctic.
